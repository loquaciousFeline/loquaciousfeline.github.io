About Us

We’re a pair of completely normal human twins separated at birth who are incredibly knowledgeable about the paranormal (which is a very normal subject to have knowledge on), driving around in a food truck offering craveable, nourishing meals that are—dare we say—addictive.

About two years ago, we noticed a substantial problem—breakfast options in the utter wilderness are horribly limited and overly expensive. Based on our natural talent, magical culinary skills, and knowledge that the most important meal of the day is always the first one, we created Bigfoot’s Breakfast to help give weary hikers and outdoorsy folk a good, nourishing, hot breakfast for affordable prices.
